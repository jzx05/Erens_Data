Output layout examples (illustrative values only; they are not ledger entries)

1) classified_events.csv
   Header fields, in this exact order, comma-separated:
   Event_ID
   Loss_Event_Category
   Business_Line
   Loss_Amount_CNY
   Example data rows:
   EX0001,EL4,BL7,1234567
   EX0002,EL6,BL5,7654321

   The header must match character for character. Every ledger Event_ID must appear
   exactly once. Category codes are EL1 through EL7. Business line codes are BL1
   through BL8. Loss amounts are the unchanged integers from the ledger.

2) capital_calculation.json
   {
     "alpha": 0.15,
     "gross_income_years": {
       "2019": 0.00,
       "2020": 0.00
     },
     "positive_year_count": 0,
     "positive_gi_sum_cny": 0.00,
     "average_positive_gi_cny": 0.00,
     "or_capital_cny": 0.00
   }

   All six keys are required and no other keys may be added. The years in
   gross_income_years must be exactly the years present in gross_income.csv.

3) execution_log.txt
   Free text. It must not be empty. A short paragraph naming the EL/BL
   classification method and the BIA averaging, alpha application and rounding
   steps is sufficient.
